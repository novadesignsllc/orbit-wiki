Enemies

Every planet has its own family of enemies, but they behave the same way everywhere. Health here is at the start of a run; it grows with the difficulty clock.

Elites

From 2:24, a gunner, spinner or tank sometimes comes in as an elite: a shimmering gold outline, 4 times the health, 8 times the XP and 4 extra gold. In the final surge they come every few waves.

Enemy bullets

Enemy bullets are always drawn on top of everything else, so you can see them. They come in small pink and big orange. Europa's ice shards and Mercury's embers are bullets too.
