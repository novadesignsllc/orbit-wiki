Guide

How to play

Controls

  • Drag anywhere to fly. Your ship follows your finger, so you never have to cover the action.
  • Your ship fires on its own while your finger is down.
  • Only the blinking core in the middle of your ship can be hit. Bullets can pass right over your wings.
  • Hold to charge your ultimate. The bar between your icons at the bottom fills while you hold. When it is full it blinks gold: lift your finger to fire it. See Ultimates.

A run

  1. Pick a planet on the star map, then a ship. Each planet has its own enemies, bosses and hazard.
  2. Kill enemies and fly over the XP gems they drop. Each level-up pauses the game and offers cards. See Leveling and cards.
  3. A boss arrives every 2:00. Beat it for a cache and a repair. If you take too long it escapes after 45 seconds. See Bosses.
  4. Max out a weapon and own its partner system, and the next cache evolves it. See Evolutions.
  5. The final minute floods the screen. Survive to 10:00 to clear the planet.

Gold you pick up is yours to keep even if you die. Spend it in the Hangar on permanent upgrades.

Your hull

Your ship has a few hull points (the pink pips at the top right). Each hit costs one and gives you 2 seconds of invincibility while the screen clears. Bosses drop a repair, the Repair card fixes 1, and some systems help: Hull Plating, Nanites and Phoenix Core.

Tips

  • Every level-up includes an upgrade for a weapon you already have, so you can always keep your main weapon growing.
  • When a card's partner is already in your build, it says "Evolves with..." and both glow in the same color.
  • Weapons can hit enemies just above the top of the screen, but only for half damage.
  • Closing the app mid-run saves it. You come back to the same spot.
