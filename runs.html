Runs

A run lasts 10:00. You start low over the planet and climb into space by the first boss.

Timeline

TimeEventOn Earth, Europa, Mars, Mercury
0:00LaunchSmall waves. Gunners, spinners and tanks join as the minutes pass (see Enemies).

10:00Planet clearedEvery enemy on screen explodes in a chain. You win.

Bosses during a run

A warning with the boss's name plays 2.5 seconds before it arrives. While a boss is on screen there are no regular waves: it is one on one. A boss you don't kill within 45 seconds escapes (a countdown shows in its last 10 seconds) and drops nothing. See Bosses.

Waves

Every few seconds a new wave comes in: a line of swarmers, a column of darts, a row of gunners, a spinner, or a tank with swarmers behind it. Waves get bigger and closer together as the run goes on. Damage and difficulty has the exact numbers.

Elites

From 2:24 on, every so often a gunner, spinner or tank comes in as an elite. Elites shimmer with a gold outline, have 4 times the health, drop 8 times the XP as a spray of gems and give 4 extra gold.

Gold and score

  • Enemies drop gold coins; fly over them to collect. Elites give 4 more and bosses drop a pile.
  • You also get 1 gold for every 20 seconds you survive.
  • You keep all your gold, even if you die.
  • Score: points for kills, 10 per second survived and 25,000 for clearing the planet.

Saving

The run is saved whenever the level-up screen is open and whenever the app goes to the background. Coming back shows "Touch to continue", and you get 1 second of invincibility when you do.
