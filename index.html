Welcome to the orBIT Wiki

orBIT is a pixel art bullet hell for phones. Pick a planet and a ship, then survive 10:00 against waves of enemies and a boss every 2:00. Every level-up gives you a choice of cards: new weapons, upgrades and systems. Max out a weapon, pair it with the right system, and a boss cache evolves it into something much stronger.
