Shockwave

Shockwave
TypeWeapon
Max level5
Color
Evolves withHull Plating
Evolution
Nova Burst
More likely onEuropa, Mars

Sends a wave rolling up the screen that smashes enemies and wipes out small bullets.

Upgrades

  • Level 2: Hits harder.
  • Level 3: Wider wave, more often.
  • Level 4: Hits harder, reaches farther.
  • Level 5: Wider, longer, harder.
