Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

orBIT is a single-player game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game keeps

  • Your save (progress, unlocks, settings and the free trial count) is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can survive reinstalling and move to your other devices. Only you and Apple can access it. The developer never receives it.
  • The free trial count is also kept in your device's Keychain.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party data collection.
  • No network requests of its own. The only online feature is Apple's in-app purchase, which Apple handles. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
