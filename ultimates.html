Ultimates

Every ship has its own ultimate. You charge it by holding: the bar fills in 28 seconds of holding. Kills add a little: 0.08% of the bar per enemy, 2% per elite and 15% per boss.

When the bar is full it blinks gold and "Let go!" shows above it. Lift your finger to fire. You stop shooting for that moment, so pick your time: right as a boss lets out a wall of bullets is a good one.
