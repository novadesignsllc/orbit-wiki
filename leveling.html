Leveling and cards

XP

Enemies drop green XP gems. Fly near them and they get pulled in; the pickup range grows with Tractor Beam and the Magnet upgrade in the Hangar. Bosses also pull in everything on screen when they die.

The first level-up takes about half a minute. After that each level needs only a little more XP than the last, while waves keep getting bigger, so level-ups come faster and faster as a run goes on.

Level-up cards

  • Each level-up pauses the game and shows 3 cards (sometimes 4 with Lucky Charm).
  • You can carry 4 weapons and 4 systems.
  • One card always upgrades a weapon you already have, as long as one can still level up.
  • The other cards are drawn at random with these odds: an upgrade for a weapon you own counts 3, an upgrade for a system you own 2, a new weapon 2, a new system 1.5.
  • Each planet's favored abilities count 2.5 times as much (see Planets).
  • When nothing is left to offer, cards become Repair (fix 1 hull) or Gold (+10).
  • A card whose partner you already own says "Evolves with...". It and the owned item share a color, the same one they'll have in your HUD.

Boss caches

A boss drops a cache. Opening it evolves a weapon if one is ready (maxed, with its partner system owned). Otherwise it is a normal set of cards. See Evolutions.

Overdrive

Once your build is maxed (4 maxed or evolved weapons, 4 maxed systems, nothing new left to take), level-ups stop pausing the game. Each one is an instant Overdrive burst:

  • +2% damage, for the rest of the run, stacking
  • +2 gold
  • every 8th Overdrive repairs 1 hull

Overdrive needs 30 XP for the first one, then 1 more for each one after. Late in a run they go off every second or two.
