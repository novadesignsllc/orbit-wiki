Evolutions

An evolution turns a maxed weapon into a much stronger version.

How to evolve

  1. Get a weapon to level 5.
  2. Own its partner system (any level).
  3. Beat a boss and open its cache. If more than one weapon is ready, the cache evolves one, and the next cache gets the next.

When a weapon is ready, its icon in the HUD blinks. A weapon and its partner share a color in the HUD (green, violet, orange or teal), and evolved pairs turn gold. Evolutions show up in the Codex once you've made them.
