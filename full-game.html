Free trial and full game

orBIT is free to download and comes with a free trial of 3 runs on Earth with the Vanguard. Every run you start counts, whether you clear it, die or quit.

The full game is a one-time purchase. No ads, and nothing that makes you stronger for money: gold and upgrades only come from playing.

What the full game includes

  • 4 full locations: Earth, Europa, Mars, Mercury, with more coming in free updates
  • All 7 ships, each with its own weapon and ultimate
  • 20 weapons, 15 systems and 15 evolutions

After the trial

When the free runs are used up, the title screen shows the full game offer every time the game opens, with buttons to buy it or restore an earlier purchase.

Your progress

Your save is kept on your iPhone and in your iCloud account, so it survives reinstalling the game and comes with you to a new device signed in to the same Apple ID. Buying on one device unlocks the others with Restore purchase.
