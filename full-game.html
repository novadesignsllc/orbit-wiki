Free and full game

orBIT is free to download. Earth is free forever, with every ship, weapon and system in the game.

The full game is a one-time purchase that opens Europa, Mars, Mercury. There are no ads, no energy timers and nothing that makes you stronger for money. Gold and upgrades only come from playing.

Previews

Launching a full-game planet without the full game plays a short preview: your ship with a strong ready-made build, a few of that planet's waves, then a fight with the Dreadnought, a boss that only appears in previews. Afterwards you can buy the full game, restore a past purchase, or go back.
