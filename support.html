Help

Support

Need help with orBIT? Email games@wilsondesign.dev and include your device model and what happened. You'll get a reply as soon as possible.

Common questions

I bought the full game but it's locked on my new device

Open the game, then tap Get the full game on the title screen (or wait for the offer screen) and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game or get a new phone?

No. Your save is kept in your iCloud account as well as on your device. Reinstall the game while signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, and your progress comes back.

How does the free trial work?

You get 3 free runs on Earth with the first ship. Every run you start counts. After that, the full game is a one-time purchase. See Free trial and full game.

The game paused when I pulled down Notification Center

That's on purpose, so you never die while you're looking at something else. Touch the screen to continue.

How do I learn how everything works?

This wiki covers every weapon, system, evolution, ship and planet.
